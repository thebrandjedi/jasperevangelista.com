01 / The Definition

What is Ghost Marketing?

Ghost Marketing is an approach to understanding and serving valuable customers whose interest, influence and buying behavior are often invisible to public engagement metrics.

They may never like your posts, leave comments or publicly declare their loyalty. Instead, they compare options privately, seek trusted recommendations, make purchases and quietly tell others.

The book Ghost Marketing: Navigating Dark Social and the Whisper Network challenges the assumption that the most visible customers are the most valuable. It asks marketers to look beyond vanity metrics and consider the people conventional engagement dashboards overlook.

This is not about hiding a brand's marketing activity. It is about recognizing customers who remain quiet in public while acting elsewhere.

02 / The Provocation

What if the people driving your growth are not the people driving your engagement?

The silent majority.

Think about your last considered purchase. You may have researched options, asked someone you trust in a private message and bought without ever responding to the brand online.

Public interaction is easy to count. Private consideration is harder to see. That gap can distort how brands define engagement, influence and customer value.

Ghost Marketing proposes a different starting point: customer behavior and business outcomes matter more than public applause.

03 / The Landscape

Dark Social. Whisper Network. Ghost signals.

Dark Social describes sharing and recommendation activity that is difficult to attribute through standard web analytics, including many private messaging and direct-sharing behaviors.

The Whisper Network is the book's lens on trusted, often private conversations that shape consideration and choice without necessarily producing a public post or review.

Ghost signals are indirect clues that quiet customers may be evaluating, returning, purchasing or advocating. They call for careful interpretation, not invasive tracking.

These ideas help marketers ask better questions, understand limitations in their data and design experiences for customers who prefer not to be visible.

06 / Common Questions

Questions worth asking.

Is Ghost Marketing the same as Dark Social?

No. Dark Social describes channels and sharing behavior that standard analytics often cannot fully attribute. Ghost Marketing is the book's broader customer-centered approach to understanding quieter forms of consideration, purchasing and recommendation.

Does Ghost Marketing mean covert advertising?

Not in the sense used in this book. Here, 'ghost' refers to customers who are largely invisible in public engagement data, not to concealed sponsorships or deceptive marketing.

Who is Ghost Marketing for?

Marketers, founders and business leaders who want to move beyond vanity metrics and better understand the customers who research, purchase and advocate without public engagement.
