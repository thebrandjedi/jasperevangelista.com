The role

I'm not a consultant.
I'm an Equalizer.

Balance isn't the middle.

Things drift. Businesses drift. Brands drift. People drift. My job isn't to hand you another framework and tell you where the middle is.

Sometimes restoring balance means pulling something back. Sometimes it means pushing harder in the other direction. Sometimes what looks like imbalance is exactly what the situation needs.

I get into the room, find where the system has drifted, connect the pieces that have stopped talking to each other, and help recalibrate what matters.

Not to make everything equal.
To make things work together again.
