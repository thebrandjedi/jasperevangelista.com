A place to recalibrate

What'syour Zero?

Zero is actually the ultimate paradox. It's not your starting point, since you've come a long way already. But after a reset, it can become a new starting point and a springboard to something greater.

Zero is actually a mindset. In a continuously fragmenting world, Zero is intentionally creating that space from which you can realign, rebalance and feel alive again. Recalibrate your brand, your career, your life, your direction.

Zero isn't the end. It's the point from which everything starts moving again.

The North Star

Everything moves.
So everything drifts.

Brands change. Businesses change. Technology accelerates. Careers evolve. People grow. The world moves around us even when we think we're standing still.

That's not necessarily a problem.

Some drift creates discovery, adaptation and growth. But move far enough without noticing, and the pieces that once worked together can begin pulling apart.

The answer isn't to stop moving. It's to notice where you are, remember what still matters, and recalibrate deliberately.

Because sometimes you return. Sometimes you keep going. And sometimes you need a new direction entirely.

My Approach

“I am not
a consultant.”

I don't believe my job is to walk into your world, apply a favorite framework and tell you what to do.

You know your business. You know your people. You know your life. You've lived with the problem longer than I have.

Sometimes what you need is someone outside it.

Someone who can ask a different question. Challenge an assumption. Share a pattern they've seen before. Point out the pitfalls ahead. Connect pieces that may have drifted apart. Or simply help you see something that's been there all along.

Sometimes, you already have a hypothesis, an instinct, even a gut feel. You just need a sounding board to pressure-test it, sharpen it, or give you the confidence to trust it.

That's closer to how I work.

I bring three decades of experience, an outsider's curiosity and a willingness to have the conversations that don't always fit neatly into a framework.

I won't make the decision for you.

But I'll stay with you long enough to help you see the road better, until you reach the point where the decision can confidently be yours.

Sometimes you don't need another answer.
You just need a little illumination.

The person behind the thinking

I've been around the block.
A few times.

For more than three decades, I've built brands and businesses from inside them.

I spent my formative years learning the craft inside two highly disciplined global marketing environments. One taught me the systems. The other tested those systems in one of the most fiercely competitive brand arenas of its time. Steel sharpens steel.

Then I took those lessons somewhere else. And somewhere else again.

From global FMCG to fashion. Media to hospitality and entertainment. Digital marketing to out-of-home technology. Telecommunications. Along the way, I moved beyond marketing into business and operational leadership too.

Different industries. Different customers. Different business models. Different competitive realities.

That's where range becomes useful.

You begin to recognize patterns across situations that look completely unrelated. You learn which principles travel and which don't. When to trust experience. When to question it. And when the smartest thing you can do is learn all over again.

Thirty years doesn't teach you everything.
It gives you enough experience to stack the odds in your favor.
